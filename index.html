About Me

Engineering Systems That Scale

Kolkata, West Bengal, India Acceleron Solutions

I am a Computer Science Engineering graduate and Software Engineer at Acceleron Solutions. My core focus is architecting scalable, resilient enterprise applications that solve mission-critical operational challenges for modern enterprises.

Much of my work centers around building enterprise-level work management, task tracking, and agile operational software — platforms engineered with the performance, reliability, and security needed to go head-to-head with Tier-1 global MNC productivity suites.

My engineering stack spans Linux environments, high-throughput REST APIs (FastAPI, Node.js, Express), modern frontends (Next.js, React), enterprise database architectures (MySQL, MongoDB, PostgreSQL), and deep integrations with SAP OData services.

Beyond full-stack engineering, I have a strong foundation in Machine Learning with TensorFlow & CNNs from my research internship at SurTech, culminating in a co-authored IEEE conference publication on agricultural ML models.
